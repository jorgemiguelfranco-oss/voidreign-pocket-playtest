Fair economy charter

Credits, Research Data and Strategic Materials are earned through play. Void Shards are optional premium currency; cosmetics never improve combat strength. Purchases and unlocks stay attached to this local save in this prototype.

Captain's ledger

Autosaves on this device. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

Debt & financing

The starting loan compounds into a survival problem, not a free counter reset. Creditor interest is charged each turn; repay principal in chunks of up to 25 credits whenever cash flow permits.

Content settings

The mature setting changes the text used for fictional violence. It does not change strategic outcomes.

Campaign rules

No account, network calls, ads, or external assets. This is a compact mobile adaptation, not a replacement for the full PC vision.
